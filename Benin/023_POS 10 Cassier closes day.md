The cash register shall be closed every day at end of (sales) business. 

 

Menu principal - Clôturer la caisse 

 

![Editkn des ventes Cbturer \'a Caisse](023_POS_10_Cassier_closes_day_000.png){width="2.9791666666666665in" height="1.5277777777777777in"}

 

 

Click \"Cloturer la caisse\" and check the results in the following screen. 

 

 

![Chromis POS - VO.94.l 15 déc. 2018 21:31:19 Clôturer la caisse Dates Séquence Rapport de paiements Paiement Espèces Imprimer Chromis pos - The New Face of Open Source POS Date de dé\... 15 déc 2018 15:5929 Date de fin Money XOF 500. If OK, then click \"Cloturer\". 

Then enter file name like \"cloturer001\". 

 

Finally, exit the program using \"Quitter\" in the main menu. 

 

Well done!
