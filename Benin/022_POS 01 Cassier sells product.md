Cassier sells products 

(anonymous, without customer data) 

 

Menu principal - Ventes  

![](022_POS_01_Cassier_sells_product_000.png){width="2.9791666666666665in" height="1.0833333333333333in"}

 

 

**Enter products** 

 

Click on products to sell  

![](022_POS_01_Cassier_sells_product_001.png){width="4.083333333333333in" height="1.0555555555555556in"}

 

They will appear in the article pane

The first position is a variable price product called \"enter price\". 

Before you select it, you first click or type the price like 5500. 

 

The second position also has variable price. 

Here, you first type the price, then \*, and then 2 (so, 2 times). 

 

The third position results just from clicking on product 1 and has a fixed price.

![](022_POS_01_Cassier_sells_product_005.png){width="5.020833333333333in" height="3.1319444444444446in"}

 

You can change e.g. the number of items and also Price+Tax. 

 

 

**Remarks** 

 

The whole process can be canceled by clicking  

![](022_POS_01_Cassier_sells_product_006.png){width="1.125in" height="0.4375in"}

 

It is also possbible to pause the sales e.g. if the customer needs to talk back to the physician. 

In the meantime you can sell products to another customer by clicking the shopping cart (left) 

![가수! 臼 +\`1](022_POS_01_Cassier_sells_product_007.png){width="1.8958333333333333in" height="0.5in"}

 

As you can see, the pin has become red and shows a \'1\' meaning that there is an open sales. 

You can click on the pin and select the open sales in order to proceed. 

 

 

**Payment** 

 

If everything is as it should be, you can type \'=\' or click  

![](022_POS_01_Cassier_sells_product_008.png){width="0.5763888888888888in" height="0.9791666666666666in"}

 

Now, enter amount that customer has given you. 

You can either type or click numbers or you click on the coins and notes. 

 

The result is the amount of change you have to give back to the customer. 

 

You can cancel the process by clicking \"Annuler\" or 

Finish by clicking \"OK\". 

 

Then save the document using a 6-digit number like 000001.

Finally, a message is shown with the amount of change. Press OK. 

 

Congratulations, your first sales is finished!
